# Smart Data Analysis and Statistics

**Biostatistics and data science for clinical research.**

Smart Data Analysis and Statistics (SDAS) develops and applies statistical methods to support clinical research and evidence generation from trials and real-world data.

## What we work on

- Clinical trial design, analysis, and simulation
- Real-world evidence and comparative effectiveness
- Evidence synthesis and meta-analysis
- Prediction models and precision medicine
- Reproducible statistical software in R

## Selected open-source work

- [metamisc](https://github.com/smartdata-analysis-and-statistics/metamisc) — tools for meta-analysis of diagnosis and prognosis research
- [precmed](https://github.com/smartdata-analysis-and-statistics/precmed) — methods for estimating and validating treatment effects
- [SimTOST](https://github.com/smartdata-analysis-and-statistics/SimTOST) — simulation-based sample-size planning for bioequivalence trials
- [bcts](https://github.com/smartdata-analysis-and-statistics/bcts) — Bayesian clinical trial simulation
